Your first visit

A first class at Pinewood is easy to settle into. Your teacher greets everyone at the door, shows you around the room and asks whether anything in your body needs special care.

Arrive
Ten minutes before class starts, which leaves time to unroll a mat and choose a spot.
Bring
A water bottle and clothes that stretch comfortably.
Space
Twelve guests at most share the room, so everyone has space to move.
