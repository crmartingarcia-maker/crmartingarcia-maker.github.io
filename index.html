Before your walk

Your first walk with Edinburgh Tours is easy to settle into. Your guide greets everyone at the meeting point, explains the route and asks whether anyone needs a slower pace or extra rest stops.

Arrive
Ten minutes before the start, which leaves time to meet your guide and the rest of the group.
Bring
Comfortable shoes, a waterproof layer and a water bottle.
Group
Fourteen guests at most join each walk, so everyone can hear the stories.
